### The Separator decision

The issue left this open and asked for it to be recorded. The rows are divided with `divide-y divide-line` on the `<dl>`, not with `Separator`. The generated `Separator` renders `<div role="separator">`, and the HTML content model for `<dl>` allows only `dt`/`dd` pairs or `<div>` groups that contain them, so a separator between rows would be invalid markup inside the list. Keeping `Separator` would have meant giving up the `<dl>`, and the name and value pairs are the reason a screen reader can announce "Origin, Huila, Colombia" together. The visual result is the same 1px `line` rule. `BagLabel` therefore imports nothing from `components/ui/separator`, which also keeps a `"use client"` module out of it.

### The logo click
