### The choices

Two were decided by Rob on 2026-11-16 before the work started and are in the issue. The rest the issue left open.

| Choice | Used | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where the heading sits | Centred: `align="center"` on `SectionHeading`. Rob's decision. | Left-aligned, the heading sat at the container's left edge and the accordion 160px to its right. | Blank items | An item with a blank question or a blank answer is left out (`hasText` from `lib/text.ts`) | The other sections get this from their blocks. This section composes the generated parts directly, so it guards its own array. A blank question would be a button with no name. |
